# Image references

Small props (the ledger, hourglass, abacus and so on) are not modelled in Blender. These sheets are their references when scene images are generated. Files are named by volume in walking order, and stored as full-resolution JPEG (quality 92).

## Where the sheets disagree with the current canon
The canon on `art-direction-grand-island-shape.html` wins. When one of these sheets is used as a reference, say what to change.
- **The ledger is a parchment scroll on two rods**, not a bound codex. `IV-ledger.jpg`, `IV-benches.jpg` and `IV-legislators.jpg` show codex books. Keep their bags, bindings and seals, but draw the ledger as a scroll.
- **The Great Round** has an inward-facing colonnaded verandah as its top tier, windows in the ring wall along it, and gates at ground level below the verandah. `IV-great-round.jpg` shows a plain low ring wall and an altar with rays in the orchestra; use it for mood, drawing style and the barred gate.
- **The Parliament's port is marble, not Cycladic whitewash.** The whitewashed village in `I-architecture.jpg` suits the hamlets of I only.
- **The cothon** in `III-architecture.jpg` is drawn as a spoked ring. The model has seven quays on a round basin around a lighthouse islet, with a walled channel to the sea.
